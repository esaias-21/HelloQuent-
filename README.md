HELLO-QUENT

Transforming anxiety into eloquence through private, intelligent speech coaching.

Live Application | MIT License


Overview

Public speaking anxiety affects individuals across all career stages and academic backgrounds. Traditional coaching programs and workshops are often cost-prohibitive, rigid in scheduling, and intimidating for individuals struggling with performance anxiety.

HELLO-QUENT is an accessible, web-based speech practice ecosystem. It provides a private, pressure-free environment equipped with scenario-based simulation engines, real-time articulation analysis, and an AI-driven coaching assistant—enabling users to refine their delivery, build resilience, and communicate effectively.

Target Audience

HELLO-QUENT is designed for anyone seeking to elevate their communication skills and build lasting speaking confidence:

Students & Researchers: Preparing for thesis defenses, capstone presentations, and classroom discussions.

Job Applicants & Career Transitioners: Practicing HR screening, behavioral interviews, and self-introductions.

Entrepreneurs & Innovators: Pitching ideas to investors, stakeholders, or audience panels.

Professionals & Executives: Structuring meeting updates, client briefings, and keynote remarks.

Anxious & Introverted Speakers: Seeking a private space to practice without audience pressure.

Core Features

1. Scenario Simulation Studio
Interactive audio drills tailored to specific communication environments:
Job Interview Simulator: Focuses on behavioral frameworks, technical articulation, and elevator pitches.
Thesis & Academic Defense: Emphasizes methodology justification, panel Q&A handling, and research clarity.
Project & Investor Pitch: Structured for 3-minute drills, value proposition delivery, and problem-solution framing.
2. Gizmo — AI Virtual Coach
Conversational guidance powered by speech analytics.
Answers user inquiries regarding speech preparation, anxiety management, and response structuring.
Delivers immediate, encouraging feedback to reinforce positive habits.
3. Non-Judgmental Feedback & Analytics
Pacing Evaluation: Monitors words-per-minute (WPM) to encourage steady delivery.
Filler-Word Detection: Identifies speech disruptions (e.g., "uhm", "like", "ah") to improve clarity.
Tone & Rhythm Tracking: Provides constructive guidance rather than numerical penalties.
4. Data Security & Backup
Local Processing: Speech analysis remains strictly within the user's browser environment.
Data Recovery: Built-in state management ensures users do not lose session records or level progress.
Business & Sustainability Model
Freemium Core Access: Essential practice scenarios, Gizmo chat assistance, and feedback metrics remain permanently free.
Budget-Friendly Pro Tier (PHP 120/month): Unlocks advanced simulation modules, unlimited AI feedback, and priority 1-on-1 human mentor bookings.
Distribution Channels: Accessible via modern web browsers on mobile and desktop devices.
Technical Architecture
Frontend: Single-Page Application (SPA) built with HTML5, CSS3, and Tailwind CSS.
Speech Engine: Web Speech API (SpeechRecognition & SpeechSynthesis) for real-time transcription and voice feedback.
State Management: LocalStorage API for encrypted data caching and offline state recovery.
Analytics: Google Analytics 4 (GA4) event tracking for feature utilization monitoring.
